#Cookie policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In one line: neither this website nor the Caesar desktop app uses analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

01What cookies are

Small files a website stores in your browser to remember you or measure you. Caesar uses them for neither.

02Cookies on these websites

  • We set no first-party or third-party cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. If something that needs consent is ever added, we will ask first.
  • The landing page and the legal documents are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any server, the host may log your IP address when it serves the pages.
  • The landing page loads its typeface from Google Fonts and its styles from cdn.tailwindcss.com: those servers receive your IP and technical request data, but we do not use it to track you.

03The desktop app

Caesar stores your preferences (theme, language, sizes) and the record that you accepted the Terms in the app's local storage, on your computer. They are not cookies, are not sent to any analytics server, and there is no telemetry. Synced collections are encrypted on your device before upload (see the Privacy Policy).

04Payments

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record. Its checkout page is theirs and may use cookies necessary to work; it is governed by their own policy. Caesar never sees or stores your card.

05Control and contact

You can clear your browser's or the app's local storage at any time. Any questions: cgarrui0301@gmail.com.
