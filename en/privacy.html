#Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Summary. Caesar keeps your data on your computer. If you create an account to sync, only an end-to-end encrypted block is uploaded to the cloud, which neither Google nor the developer can read. There is no analytics, telemetry or tracking cookies.

01Data controller

Cesar Garrido, natural person. Contact: cecegames.app@gmail.com. See also the Legal notice.

02Guest mode: data only on your device

You can use Caesar without an account (guest mode). In that case nothing is sent to any server of the developer: your data stays on your computer.

The HTTP requests you send go directly from your computer to the destination you specify, without passing through the developer's servers. The interface does not make network connections itself: everything goes through the application core (Rust).

03Data stored on your computer

Caesar stores on your device, unencrypted:

  • Open requests, including headers, body and credentials you type into them.
  • Collections.
  • Variables (they may contain secrets).
  • Preferences: theme, language, fonts, widths, animations and auto-update.
  • A "first time" flag (welcome screen).

They are stored in the app's localStorage and in a datos.json file (with auxiliary .bak copies when remote data is downloaded and .tmp while writing) in the app's data folder, in plain text. Exports and imports are only read or written where you choose.

Two items are stored in the operating system keychain: the data key (AES-256, 32 random bytes; never written to disk or shown in the interface) and the Firebase session token (refresh token). The GitHub token is not stored. More detail in Security.

If you decide to create an account to sync between computers, this data is processed:

Google/Firebase sees your email, your uid, your IP when connecting, dates and the blob size, but not its content: the blob is end-to-end encrypted with a key that exists only on your computers. If you lose the key and all your computers, the cloud data is unrecoverable and the developer cannot help you.

05Updates

Caesar checks for a new version by querying github.com/cesargarridor/caesar/releases (latest.json file, signed with minisign). Unless you turn it off in Settings, the update is installed automatically. When connecting, GitHub may log your IP under its own policy.

06No analytics or cookies

Caesar includes no analytics, telemetry, tracking cookies or automatic crash reports. This website is static, uses no cookies or scripts, and is hosted on GitHub Pages.

07Purposes and legal basis

  • Providing the account and sync service you request: performance of the requested service (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Service security and abuse prevention: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

No automated decisions or profiling are made.

08Recipients

Google (Firebase Authentication and Firestore) acts as provider of the account and storage service. GitHub (Microsoft) is involved only if you choose that sign-in, and also as host of the application releases. No data is sold or passed to third parties for commercial purposes.

09International transfers

Firestore data is located in the Europe multi-region (eur3), but Google may process data outside the EU under standard contractual clauses or the applicable privacy framework. GitHub (Microsoft) processes your data only if you choose that sign-in.

10Retention

Your account data and the encrypted blob are kept as long as your account exists. When you delete it (Settings → Sync) they are removed immediately, except technical copies Google may keep during its retention cycle. See Delete your data.

11Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to cecegames.app@gmail.com (guide in Delete your data). If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

12Minors

Caesar is not directed at children under 14. Do not use the account if you are younger.

13Payments

Sync is free for now and Caesar does not process payments. If payments are added in the future, you will be notified and this policy will be updated before they take effect. If a new provider is introduced (for example hosting or payments), it will be listed here.

14Changes to this policy

If it changes, the date above will be updated and, for relevant changes, notice will be given in the app or the repository.
