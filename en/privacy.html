#Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary. In guest mode your data stays on your computer. An account is optional. The Pro plan is a paid subscription with automatic renewal that enables sync; you can cancel whenever you want (refunds and cancellation). Only an end-to-end encrypted block is uploaded to the cloud, which neither Google nor the developer can read. Payments are charged by Lemon Squeezy: Caesar never sees your card. There is no analytics, telemetry or tracking cookies, your data is not used for AI, and no data is sold.

01Data controller

Cesar Garrido, natural person. Contact: cecegames.app@gmail.com. See also the Legal notice.

02Guest mode: data only on your device

You can use Caesar without an account (guest mode, free). In that case nothing is sent to any server of the developer: your data stays on your computer.

The HTTP requests you send go directly from your computer to the destination you specify, without passing through the developer's servers. The interface does not make network connections itself: everything goes through the application core (Rust).

03Data stored on your computer

Caesar stores on your device, unencrypted:

  • Open requests, including headers, body and credentials you type into them.
  • Collections.
  • Variables (they may contain secrets).
  • Preferences: theme, language, fonts, widths, animations and auto-update.
  • A "first time" flag (welcome screen).

They are stored in the app's localStorage and in a datos.json file (with auxiliary .bak copies when remote data is downloaded and .tmp while writing) in the app's data folder, in plain text. Exports and imports are only read or written where you choose.

Two items are stored in the operating system keychain: the data key (AES-256, 32 random bytes; never written to disk or shown in the interface) and the Firebase session token (refresh token). The GitHub token is not stored. More detail in Security.

05Updates, installer and code signing

On startup, and when you click "Check for updates", Caesar checks for a new version by querying github.com/cesargarridor/caesar/releases (latest.json file, signed with minisign). GitHub receives your IP address and a standard HTTPS request (usual headers such as the user agent) and may log them under its own policy; Caesar sends no identifiers, usage data or content of yours. Unless you turn it off in Settings → Updates, the update is downloaded and installed automatically.

The installer and binaries do not collect data or contact the developer's servers, and there is no telemetry. When you download the installer from GitHub Releases, GitHub sees that download (your IP and the request) under its own policy.

Code signing (status as of November 13, 2026): macOS builds are signed and notarized with Apple; Windows and Linux builds are not code-signed yet (Windows may show SmartScreen or "unknown publisher"); updates are signed with minisign and the app verifies the signature. Details in the code signing policy.

06No analytics or cookies

Caesar includes no analytics, telemetry, tracking cookies or automatic crash reports. This website is static, uses no cookies or scripts, and is hosted on GitHub Pages.

07Purposes and legal basis

  • Providing the account, Pro and sync service you request, and managing your subscription: performance of the contract (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Keeping the record of acceptance of terms and age, and the data required by tax and consumer law: legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c GDPR) and, to prove acceptance, legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).
  • Service security, abuse prevention and code-redemption limits: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

No automated decisions or profiling are made.

09International transfers

Firestore data is located in the Europe multi-region (eur3), but Google, Cloudflare, Lemon Squeezy and GitHub (Microsoft) may process data outside the EU or EEA, in particular in the USA. They do so under standard contractual clauses or the applicable privacy framework, and each provider offers its own data processing terms.

10Retention

  • Account data and encrypted blob: as long as your account exists.
  • Acceptance record and subscription data: deleted when you delete the account.
  • Accounts without a plan: if you do not activate Pro within 7 days of signing up, the account is deleted automatically.
  • Code-redemption counters: deleted after 24 hours.
  • Billing and invoicing data: kept by Lemon Squeezy for as long as tax law requires.

When you delete the account (Settings → Account and subscription), Caesar data is removed immediately, except technical copies Google may keep during its retention cycle. See Delete your data.

11Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to cecegames.app@gmail.com (guide in Delete your data). If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

12Minors

Caesar is only for people aged 14 or over. The app asks you to confirm your age and no parental consent is offered, so it is not for children under 14. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if we detect an account of a child under 14, it is deleted.

13Payments and subscription

Pro payments are charged by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record: it handles charging, taxes, invoices and payment data. Caesar never sees your card. The subscription renews automatically and you can cancel whenever you want. Full conditions in the Terms and in Refunds and cancellation.

14Artificial intelligence and marketing

Caesar includes no AI features and does not use users' data to train models.

No marketing emails are sent. If they ever were, it would only be with your separate, optional consent and with an unsubscribe link.

15Changes to this policy

If it changes, the date above and the terms and privacy version (current: 2026-11-13) will be updated. For relevant changes, the app will ask you to accept them again.
