#Refunds and cancellation policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary. You can cancel whenever you want, from the app, and keep Pro until the end of the paid period. If you ask for a refund within 14 days of any charge, we give you everything back, no explanation needed.

01Who can ask

Anyone who has paid for Pro, for the first payment or for a renewal. "Pro forever" codes (Compas plan) are redeemed in the app and involve no charge by Caesar. If you are a consumer in the EU, your 14-day right of withdrawal is also respected.

02How to cancel the subscription

  • In the app: Settings → Account and subscription → "Manage subscription".
  • At Lemon Squeezy: from the customer portal or the link in your receipts.

Cancelling is as easy as signing up. When you cancel you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for and are not charged again. Deleting the account from the app also cancels the subscription. You can cancel without deleting your account.

0314-day refund

If you ask within 14 days of any charge (first payment or renewal), we refund the full amount, with no explanation needed.

04How to ask

Write to cecegames.app@gmail.com with the email you paid with, or ask Lemon Squeezy directly from your receipt or its customer portal. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record.

05Where the money goes

It is returned to the same payment method you used, normally within 5-10 business days (the final time depends on your bank).

06After 14 days

After 14 days from the charge, periods already enjoyed are not refunded, unless the law requires it. You can still cancel so there are no future charges.

07If the service stops being provided

If the sync service stopped being provided, the unused part of the paid period will be refunded.

08More information

Full conditions in the Terms of use and payment data handling in the Privacy policy.
