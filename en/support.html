#Support

Last updated: 15 November 2026

How to get help. Write to cgarrui0301@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub. Caesar is maintained by one person: I reply as soon as I can, with no guaranteed response time.

01Where to write

  • Bugs and suggestions: GitHub Issues. It is public, so do not paste keys or private data.
  • Account, payments and refunds: cgarrui0301@gmail.com. Include the email you signed up with. Read the refund policy first.
  • Privacy and personal data: cgarrui0301@gmail.com (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability). You can also delete your data yourself.
  • Security: see the security page before reporting a vulnerability.

02What to include

  • Operating system and Caesar version (Settings → Privacy & licence).
  • What you expected, what happened and the steps to reproduce it.
  • Screenshots or the error text. Always remove keys, tokens and personal data.

03Common problems

  • Install warnings: see the download page.
  • Linux Snap: if the keyring gives an AppArmor error, run sudo snap connect caesar:password-manager-service.
  • I lost my devices: synced data is encrypted with your key and cannot be recovered without it (see privacy).
