#Caesar

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Caesar is a Fair Source (FSL license, not open source) desktop API client (Tauri). Here are its legal documents: what data it stores, how it is protected, your rights and the terms of use.

Download Caesar

In one line: the requests you send go straight from your computer to their destination, never through the developer's servers. There is no analytics, telemetry or tracking cookies. The account and sync are optional; without an account (guest mode) nothing is sent to any server of ours.
