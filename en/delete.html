#Delete your data and exercise your rights

Last updated: November 11, 2026

01Sign out

Signing out deletes the session token from the keychain. It does not delete your account, your cloud data or your local data.

02Delete the account and cloud data

In the app: Settings → Sync → Delete account. This removes:

  • The sync/main document (the encrypted blob).
  • The subscription state, if any existed.
  • The Firebase account.
  • The session token in the keychain.

The effect is immediate, except technical copies Google may keep during its retention cycle.

It does not delete local data. Your requests, collections and variables remain on your computer, and so does the data key in the keychain.

03Delete local data

To remove what Caesar stores on your computer:

  • Delete the datos.json file (and its .bak and .tmp copies) from the app data folder.
  • Clear the app data (localStorage) using your system's data option or by deleting the app data folder.
  • Remove Caesar's entries from the operating system keychain (the data key and, if it remains, the session token).
  • Delete any exports you saved wherever you chose.

04Exercise your GDPR rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to cecegames.app@gmail.com. State the right you are exercising and your account email. We may ask you to confirm your identity from that same email.

We will reply within the one-month period set by the GDPR.

RightHow
AccessWe can tell you what is on record for your account: email, uid, dates and blob size. Only you can read the content: it is encrypted and we cannot decrypt it.
RectificationYour email is managed with your sign-in provider; write to us if you need help.
ErasureUse Settings → Sync → Delete account or ask by email.
PortabilityYour data is on your computer; export it from the app whenever you want.
Restriction and objectionWrite to us stating the reason.

If you are not satisfied with the response, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
