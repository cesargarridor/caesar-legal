#Delete your data and exercise your rights

Last updated: November 13, 2026

01Sign out

Signing out deletes the session token from the keychain. It does not delete your account, your cloud data or your local data, and it does not cancel the subscription.

02Cancel the subscription without deleting the account

You can cancel Pro without deleting anything: Settings → Account and subscription → "Manage subscription", or from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. See Refunds and cancellation.

03Delete the account and cloud data

In the app: Settings → Account and subscription → Delete account. This:

  • Cancels the active subscription at Lemon Squeezy.
  • Deletes the sync/main document (the encrypted blob).
  • Deletes the acceptance record and subscription data.
  • Deletes the Firebase account.
  • Deletes the session token from the keychain.

The effect is immediate, except technical copies Google may keep during its retention cycle. Billing and invoicing data is kept by Lemon Squeezy for as long as tax law requires. Accounts without a plan are deleted automatically after 7 days.

It does not delete local data. Your requests, collections and variables stay on your computer, and so does the data key in the keychain.

04Delete local data

To remove what Caesar stores on your computer:

  • Delete the datos.json file (and its .bak and .tmp copies) from the app's data folder.
  • Clear the app data (localStorage) with the system's data option or by deleting the app's data folder.
  • Remove Caesar's entries from the operating system keychain (the data key and, if left, the session token).
  • Delete any exports you saved where you chose.

05Download your data and portability

In the app: Settings → Account and subscription → "Download my data". It generates a JSON file with your account, your plan, your acceptance record and your local content, in a structured format you can keep or take to another service.

06Exercise your GDPR rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection by writing to cecegames.app@gmail.com. State the right you are exercising and your account email. We may ask you to confirm your identity from that same email. If the app is no longer available, write to that email too.

We will reply within the one-month period set by the GDPR.

RightHow
AccessWe can tell you what we hold for your account: email, uid, plan, subscription status, acceptance record, dates and blob size. Only you can read the content: it is encrypted and we cannot decrypt it.
RectificationYour email is managed at your sign-in provider; billing data, at Lemon Squeezy. If you need help, write to us.
ErasureUse Settings → Account and subscription → Delete account or ask by email.
PortabilityUse "Download my data" (JSON) or export from the app whenever you want.
Restriction and objectionWrite to us stating the reason.

If you disagree with the reply, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
